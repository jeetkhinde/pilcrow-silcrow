Two deferred types:
  • Deferred<T> — wraps a scalar future. T must implement Display. Streams as window.__pilcrow_deferred(key, value). Renders empty in the shell.
  • DeferredHtml — wraps a future returning an HTML string. Streams as window.__pd(slot, html). .with_loading(html) shows a skeleton while resolving.
Unlike STREAMING: each field resolves and patches independently — rendered_at uses regular Askama {% raw %}{{ }}{% endraw %} interpolation (immediate, in the shell) while the two deferred fields arrive later at their own pace. Deferred fields are also compatible with ISR and SSG — add REVALIDATE to this page and the deferred fields still work correctly.
